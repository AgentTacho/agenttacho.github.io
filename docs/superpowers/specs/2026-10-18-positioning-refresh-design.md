# Design: Landing Site Positioning Refresh

**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Status:** approved (brainstorm complete; pending plan)
**Repo:** agentmeter-site (the AgentTacho landing page)

## Intent and Why

The landing page sells AgentTacho on things that are not actually
differentiators and buries the things that are. "Menu bar app for macOS"
and "one line of your menu bar" describe every menu bar app. Meanwhile the
real reasons someone chooses AgentTacho are underplayed:

1. **The at-a-glance budget read.** How much of my budgets and limits is
   left, and whether it lasts to the reset at my current pace.
2. **One consolidated overview.** Every account and model in a single view
   at once, not scattered across separate dropdowns the way CodexBar does.
3. **Many accounts, one budget.** A company can put several models and
   accounts, even several on the same provider, under one budget, and the
   app also covers pay-per-token accounts.

The brand is a tachometer: one focused instrument on the dashboard, not
the whole cockpit. The target reader is someone overwhelmed by CodexBar, or
someone who just wants a simple way to watch their usage. The feeling to
convey is calm simplicity, and that is carried as much by the screenshots
and an uncluttered page as by the words. Simplicity is shown, not shouted:
we do not put "simple" in large type, because the CodexBar-weary are
suspicious of that claim.

## Goals

- Replace the generic hero slogan and tagline with copy that leads on
  budgets and the many-accounts-one-budget strength.
- Give "several accounts on one provider" prominent placement as a
  tagline-level strength.
- Make the comparison table lead on real differentiators (one overview,
  budgets, shared budget), not on "one line".
- Make the comparison's yes/no cells consistent, with a checkmark always
  meaning the good thing.
- Remove claims that are self-evident or not differentiating.
- Carry the calm/gauge tone through the whole page.

## Non-Goals and Anti-Goals

- **Not** a visual redesign of the page. Layout, colors, and components
  stay; this is copy, one new section, and comparison-table content.
- **Not** naming CodexBar as "overwhelming" in the hero. The contrast is
  made by showing simplicity and by one pointed "not a dozen dropdowns"
  beat, not by disparagement. The comparison stays fair and dated.
- **Anti-goal:** shouting "simple" in the headline. If the page ends up
  claiming simplicity more than demonstrating it, it has failed even if
  every word is accurate.
- **Anti-goal:** shipping comparison claims about competitors that are not
  re-verified against their current docs.

## Phasing

The screenshots are generated by an `agentmeter` capture run (see Cross-Repo
Dependencies), and every screenshot stem in this repo is currently a
placeholder. `Shot.astro` throws at build time if a stem's `.webp` is
missing, so any section pointing at a not-yet-captured stem cannot ship.
Therefore the refresh ships in two phases:

- **Phase 1 (site-only, ships now):** hero copy, `config.yaml` description,
  the comparison flip and new row, the tone pass, and the regenerated
  `og.png`. None of these depend on new captures.
- **Phase 2 (after the capture lands):** the "several accounts, one
  provider" spotlight band, once an `agentmeter` capture produces a suitable
  screenshot (and the pill render and #24 crops are in). The capture run is
  the maintainer's action per `agentmeter`'s AGENTS.md.

## Decisions

### 1. Hero (`src/components/agenttacho/Hero.astro`, `src/config.yaml`) — Phase 1

New top-of-page copy (direction B from the brainstorm):

- **Eyebrow:** Budgets and limits for coding agents
- **Headline:** One glance. Every account.
- **Tagline:** See what's left and whether it lasts to the reset, across
  all your models and accounts, including several on the same provider.

The `metadata.description` in `src/config.yaml` (currently "Plan limits and
budgets for Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode, in one line of your menu bar.")
is updated to match the new tagline's substance and drop "one line".

The SEO **`metadata.title` stays "AgentTacho: usage at a glance"** (title,
template, and OG `site_name`/title unchanged). This is a deliberate call:
keep the established, clean title for SEO stability even though the hero H1
is now "One glance. Every account." Only the eyebrow, H1, and tagline copy
change.

### 2. Drop "signed and notarized" from the hero meta line

The hero meta line currently reads "Free and open source · macOS 14+ on
Apple Silicon · signed and notarized · or Homebrew". Remove "signed and
notarized"; it is expected and self-evident. The remaining items and the
Homebrew `<details>` stay.

The "Verified updates" entry in `smallThings` (`src/data/features.ts`)
**stays** (it describes the one-click, integrity-checked update flow, which
is a real capability, not a trust badge). This is a call made during the
brainstorm; revisit if it reads as belaboring notarization.

### 2b. Social card (`scripts/make-og.sh`, `src/assets/images/og.png`) — Phase 1

`make-og.sh` renders the slogan text directly into the 1200x630 card
("Usage at a glance." / "Nothing more." / "Plan limits and budgets ... in
one line of your menu bar."). After the hero copy changes, this card
contradicts the page. Update the headline and sub-text baked into
`make-og.sh` to match the new hero (H1 and tagline from Decision 1, written
dash-free), then regenerate `og.png`. The card's layout, colors, mascot,
and menu-bar shot stay.

### 3. Spotlight band: "Several accounts, one provider" — Phase 2

A new wide feature block of its own, placed **between `MenuBarStrip` and
`FeatureGrid`** in `src/pages/index.astro`. It is not a grid cell: the
nine-card 3x3 grid stays intact and equal-sized. The band gives this
strength more weight than a grid cell would, matching its tagline-level
importance. **Ships in Phase 2**, after an `agentmeter` capture provides a
screenshot that shows multiple same-provider accounts together (the demo
scenario already has such accounts: a Codex subscription plus an OpenAI API
account, and a Claude Max subscription plus Claude-via-Vertex).

To avoid echoing the existing "Shared budgets" feature card, which sits
just below in the grid, the two are deliberately differentiated: the **band
is about several accounts on the same provider, all in one view** (for
example two accounts on one provider seen together), while the **"Shared
budgets" card stays about a company budget shared across different accounts
or people**. Word both so they do not repeat each other; both cards stay in
the grid (decision: differentiate, keep both).

- Content: several accounts on the same provider (for example two Anthropic
  accounts, or a personal and a work account), all rolled into the one
  overview and, where wanted, under one budget. Ties back to the "one view,
  not scattered" idea.
- It has a screenshot. The exact shot is a builder decision; a natural
  candidate is the dropdown or details view showing multiple same-provider
  accounts together. If no existing captured stem shows this, it is a new
  stem and therefore a companion `agentmeter` capture change (see
  Cross-Repo Dependencies).
- Visual treatment follows the existing section components (reuse the
  `MenuBarStrip` wide-panel look or a `FeatureGrid`-style card scaled up);
  the builder picks, keeping it consistent with the page.

### 4. Comparison table (`src/data/comparison.ts`, `src/components/agenttacho/Compare.astro`) — Phase 1

- **Retire the "one line" selling point.** Remove "keeps the menu bar to
  one line" from the intro paragraph, and replace the "Menu bar" row
  (`'One line, entries you choose'`, etc.) with a row that states the real
  differentiator: everything in one consolidated view at once, versus
  scattered items or dropdowns. Working label: "Everything in one view".
  The intro paragraph is rewritten to lead on budgets, shared budgets, and
  the one-overview idea, while staying fair (CodexBar still covers far more
  providers).
- **Yes/no symbols everywhere, checkmark = good.** Flip rows where "No" is
  the desirable answer so a checkmark means the good thing:
  - "Reads browser cookies" becomes **"No browser cookies"**: AgentTacho
    and ccusage (which do not read cookies) show a checkmark; the "Optional"
    entries stay as the word "Optional" (a genuine middle state), or a
    partial mark if the builder prefers, consistently applied.
  - "Telemetry" becomes **"No telemetry"**: AgentTacho (None) shows a
    checkmark; "Anonymous daily heartbeat" shows the cross (it has
    telemetry); "Not stated" stays the word "Not stated" (unknown is not a
    yes or a no).
  - Remaining genuine yes/no cells (for example the `false` cells in
    "Dollar budgets you set") render as the cross icon as they do today.
  - Descriptive rows (Form, Agents and providers, and the text cells of the
    budget rows) stay as words; they are not yes/no.
- `Compare.astro` already renders `true`/`false` as the check / minus
  icons. Flipping a row means changing the data values in `comparison.ts`
  (and the row label), not the rendering, except for any new "partial"
  state the builder introduces, which needs a third icon in `Compare.astro`.

### 5. Tone pass — Phase 1

Carry the calm/gauge framing through existing section copy where it is
cheap to do so (headings and intros), without rewriting the whole page.
The "simple, only what's needed" positioning lives in the tone and in the
"not a dozen dropdowns" comparison beat, not in a new "simple!" banner.

## Cross-Repo Dependencies (in `agentmeter`, not this repo)

These cannot be done in the site repo; the screenshots are generated by
`agentmeter/scripts/screenshots/capture.sh` and written into
`src/assets/agenttacho/`.

1. **Readable dialog shots.** Already captured in `agentmeter` brainstorm
   #24 (`brainstorm/24-readable-dialog-shots.md`, committed). The simple,
   readable screenshots this design relies on depend on that work.
2. **Pill, not colored text, for highlighted menu-bar levels.** The
   highlighted entries in the menu-bar bar (for example a budget past 100%)
   should render with a colored background pill, not colored text, in the
   demo bar used for capture. See the reference image provided in the
   brainstorm. This is a demo-render / scenario change in `agentmeter`
   (the `menubar` stem and any shot showing the bar), to be done alongside
   or after #24. Flag it to the `agentmeter` maintainer; it is a sibling
   item to #24.
3. **A spotlight screenshot** for the "several accounts, one provider"
   band, if no existing stem shows multiple same-provider accounts (see
   Decision 3). If a new stem is needed, it is added to `STEMS` and the
   capture in `agentmeter` per that repo's AGENTS.md.

## What's Left to the Builder

- Exact wording of the spotlight band's heading and body (within the intent
  in Decision 3), and which screenshot it uses.
- The exact rewritten comparison intro paragraph.
- The new comparison row's label and, crucially, its **verified** cell
  values for every competitor (see Open Questions), plus whether to keep or
  drop a separate menu-bar row.
- Whether the "Optional" cookie cells use the word or a third "partial"
  icon, and, if an icon, adding it to `Compare.astro`.
- Minor tone edits to existing section headings/intros.
- House rules apply to all prose: no em-dash or en-dash (use commas,
  parentheses, or separate sentences); run human-facing marketing copy
  through the prose plugin's humanizer per the project conventions.

## Open Questions

- **Comparison facts for the new "one view" row (Phase 1 blocker for that
  row).** What are the accurate, current values for CodexBar, Claude Usage
  Tracker, ClaudeBar, and ccusage on "everything in one consolidated view at
  once"? These must be checked against each project's README/docs and the
  table's `checked` date bumped before the row ships. The builder (or a
  research pass) verifies; this design does not invent them. The rest of the
  comparison work (the flip, the intro rewrite) does not depend on this.
- **Which screenshot the spotlight band uses (Phase 2).** The demo scenario
  has same-provider accounts, so the content exists, but the exact stem (an
  existing one such as `dropdown`, or a new purpose-made stem) is decided
  when the `agentmeter` capture is run. The band ships only once that image
  exists.
